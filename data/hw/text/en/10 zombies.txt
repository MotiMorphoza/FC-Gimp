zombies – zombies  
zombies everywhere  
beware of the zombies  
zombies here and there  

with masks and gloves  
you cannot see the face  
the zombies – the zombies  
like rats in a race  

they follow with sorrow  
you must obey the rules  
if I’m a zombie, you’re a zombie  
we do not need no proof  

you spit on them, they say it’s rain  
try to wake them — it’s in vain  
too late for you, too late for them  
the world has gone insane
