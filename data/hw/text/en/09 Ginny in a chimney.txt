TITLE: Ginny in a chimney

BODY:

You might have seen me or felt me,
but you don’t really care.
I’m not an illusion,
yet you treat me like air.
But I’m not going away — I’m here to stay.
Yes, I represent your culture today.

All your needs of consuming,
much more than you need.
The monster inside
just wants you to feed
your meaningless life
and the fear of death.
Though the cravings you crave
bring you closer to the grave.

I’m not like terror,
I work really slow.
You don’t even notice
while your lungs grow a hole.
Ohhhhh, your misery, man —
it will never come to an end.
You’re chasing for fun
while losing a friend.

Some look for a solution,
but there is no hope.
They call me pollution,
and I’ve tightened the rope.
