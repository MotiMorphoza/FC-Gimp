We were Adam and Eve.

Monkeys.

A deer and a gazelle.

Prehistoric man, ancient woman.
Native American warriors.
Children.

We built a microcosm
and we invented stories.

A hundred square meters of forest
felt like a whole continent.

We wandered through marshlands,
Japanese winter gardens,
moonrise heights.

We were synchronized.

Between conifers and oaks,
with our senses enhanced.

Half a silvery face
in the full moonlight,
half a glowing face
lit by the fire.

We made love.
