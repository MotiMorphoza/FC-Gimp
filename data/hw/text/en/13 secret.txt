BODY:

The Yin of the Yang,
she’s so thin and so young.
The Yang of the Yin —
there are no morals within.

No holiness between those sheets,
and behind her
rests the holy shit.

Deception and manipulation —
such a combination.
Without your acceptance
they stole your attention.

Using the same nasty tools
just to use us as their tools,
to blind you,
anesthetize you,
take control over what defines you.

“Victory,” you pray and pay,
and they are here to stay:
the good policeman and the bad,
the devil and his dad.

Though you
are
already
dead.
