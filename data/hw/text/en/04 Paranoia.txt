TITLE: Paranoia

BODY:

Sometimes it slips into my head
the thought..

that I'm as boring as a dead
man, who has nothing more to give

or that I might have done something
that you can't forgive

so I wait for a sign
just to know that it's fine

that I'm not too stinky or dirty
too intense or too "thirsty"

so I wait and I wait and I wait
and for a moment
I'm almost losing my faith

but then you write

it's alright
all is bright
there's no fight
hold on tight
catch the flight

I will survive
the night.
