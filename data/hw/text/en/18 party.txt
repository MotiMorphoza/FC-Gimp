TITLE: The party of all nightmares

BODY:

At that party,
I felt like I was standing
in front of a machine gun.

Every face was like a bullet
of memory,
piercing more
and more emptiness into me.

If this is hell,
then it’s not so bad.

I’m waiting for the moment
I’ll wake up, but
it’s not a dream—
it’s a nightmare.

The party of all nightmares
