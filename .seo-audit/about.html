About me

Moti – storyteller.

I traveled across Europe and Asia, working in hostels, agricultural farms and performing on the streets.
I spent over a decade in Poland without documents doing theater,
a period I call “My Sweet jail”.
It's when i stoped asking “Where are you from?”
and became interested in “Who are you?”.

The camera opened a new dimension in my life.
From standing in the spotlights as a performer, I suddenly found myself hiding in the shadows behind the lens, documenting a reality filtered through my personal perspective.
The streets remained my favorite stage, and I developed a passion for capturing small conceptual collisions between people, objects, and symbols.

I create outside the institutions of the art world and without formal training. The work develops intuitively — through observation
and through a desire to dismantle the stories society tells itself.
Technical perfection is secondary to me.
The story inside the image is what matters most.

MotoSynteza is an attempt to
“make the usual unusual”
