Smart Times

He sees you, he knows you. He passes through walls. Processing your data, he answers your calls. He listens and records, he knows where you are. Without him, my friend, you won’t get very far. He made you addicted — life is pink as a gum — that sticks to your pants.. yes, it all was a scam. He’s smart, you are not. You can’t think on your own. Oh no.. you’re a victim of your cellular phone.

Phones give the gallery a recurring visual anchor.
